Meridian FC completed one of the most dramatic comebacks of the season on Saturday, overturning a two-goal half-time deficit with three goals in the final twenty minutes to win 3-2.

The visitors looked comfortable at the break, controlling possession and limiting Meridian to shots from distance. A tactical switch at half-time changed the shape of the game entirely, pushing an extra body forward and forcing errors from a previously composed backline.

## The turning point

The equaliser arrived in the 78th minute, followed by the winner deep into stoppage time — a finish that sent the home crowd into scenes rarely seen at the stadium this season.
