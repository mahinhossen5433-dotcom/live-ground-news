Harborview FC are closing in on the signing of Union City's influential midfielder, with a fee understood to be agreed in principle between the two clubs, sources close to the negotiations say.

The move has been in the works for several weeks, with Harborview's recruitment team tracking the player's performances since the start of the season. A medical is expected to be scheduled before the weekend, pending final paperwork.

## Why the move makes sense

Harborview's manager has been vocal about needing a ball-progressing presence in midfield, and the incoming player's passing numbers rank among the best in the league this season. The club is also said to be exploring add-on clauses tied to appearances and continental qualification.

Union City, for their part, are believed to be lining up a replacement should the deal go through, with at least two names already on their shortlist.

Further updates on the medical and formal announcement will follow as the story develops.
