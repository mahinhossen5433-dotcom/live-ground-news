Coastal City welcome Ironbridge United this weekend in a derby that carries early title implications, with both sides unbeaten through the opening rounds of the season.

## Key battle

The midfield tussle is expected to define the game. Coastal City's press has forced turnovers in dangerous areas all season, while Ironbridge have leaned on quick transitions to punish exactly that kind of aggression.

## Team news

Coastal City are expected to be at full strength, while Ironbridge are managing a knock picked up in training — a late fitness test looms for one of their key attackers.

## Prediction

Expect a tight, cagey first half followed by a more open finish as fatigue sets in. Recent meetings between these two have been decided by fine margins, and there is little reason to expect anything different this time.
