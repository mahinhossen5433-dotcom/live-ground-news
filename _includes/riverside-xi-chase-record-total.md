Riverside XI produced the highest successful run-chase in the club's history on Tuesday, overhauling Northgate Warriors' imposing 312 with four balls to spare to seal the three-match series 2-0.

Chasing a target that had never been chased down at this ground before, Riverside lost early wickets inside the powerplay but rebuilt through a composed middle-order partnership before an unbeaten century in the final overs finished the job in style.

## A chase for the ages

The chase looked in trouble at 84 for 3 in the 14th over, with the required rate climbing past ten an over. What followed was a counter-attacking display that shifted momentum decisively — boundaries came in clusters, and by the 40th over the equation had been reduced to a formality.

Northgate's bowlers, who had defended totals above 300 twice already this season, found no answers in the dying overs as the ball repeatedly found the gaps in the deep.

## What it means

The result hands Riverside an unassailable series lead heading into the final fixture, and further strengthens their case for a top-two finish in the standings. Northgate will regroup with a dead rubber to salvage pride, but questions about their death-overs bowling depth are likely to follow them into the next assignment.

> "We back ourselves to chase anything on this ground — tonight the belief just showed up at the right time," said the Riverside XI captain after the match.
